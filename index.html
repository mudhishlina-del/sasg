Harmonic Modeling of Air-Conditioning Demand Using Peak Shaving and Fourier Decomposition Techniques

Riyadh’s summer load peaks in the early afternoon, when air conditioning runs hardest. We broke the daily curve into Fourier harmonics and used them to plan which hours give up load and which take it, then compared that plan with plain peak shaving under the same energy budget.
